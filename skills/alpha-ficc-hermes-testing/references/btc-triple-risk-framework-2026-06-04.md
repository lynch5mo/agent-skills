1. Liquidity contraction (Treasury TGA)
2. Narrative collapse (AI vs Gold competition)
3. Inflation trap (oil → breakeven → real rates → zero-yield assets)

## 1. Liquidity Layer — TGA as Leading Indicator

### Data
